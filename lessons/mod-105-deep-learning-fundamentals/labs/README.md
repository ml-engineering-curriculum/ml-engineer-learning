# Labs for mod-105-deep-learning-fundamentals (Deep Learning Fundamentals with PyTorch)

Labs are short, guided, hands-on sessions. Each one isolates one piece
of the module — the autograd engine, the training loop's correctness,
or the learning-rate schedule — so that the mechanics are visible and
testable before they appear inside the full exercises.

| # | Lab | Duration | Companion chapters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | [Autograd and the four-line loop, by hand](lab-01-autograd-and-the-four-line-loop.md) | 30–45 min | 02, 03 |
| 02 | [Overfit a batch of 8: training-loop smoke tests](lab-02-overfit-a-batch-of-eight.md) | 45–60 min | 03, 02, 05 |
| 03 | [Visualising learning-rate schedules](lab-03-visualising-lr-schedules.md) | 30–45 min | 08, 03 |

Labs are deliberately distinct from the module's exercises (which
train full MLPs, CNNs, and Transformers end to end on real datasets).
A lab makes one primitive concrete; an exercise wires several of them
together on a realistic workload.

Suggested order: do each lab shortly **after** reading its companion
chapter, and **before** starting the exercise that uses that piece.
